## 1. Core commitments

1.1 We sell **evidence, not assurances**. Where we claim something works, the claim is accompanied by the evidence that establishes it, or is labelled as unverified.

1.2 **Prices are offers/targets, not proof of customer-production validation** of any capability. Capability status is disclosed per offering in the applicable Schedule.

1.3 **No money is taken without recorded customer consent.** Every payment is preceded by a consent record (see CONSENT-RECORD-SPEC.md) referencing the offer, price, and terms as presented. No consent record, no payment.

1.4 We do not silently change scope. Consequential changes go through a signed variation.

## 2. Orders, consent, and payment

2.1 **An order exists when** the customer confirms acceptance of a written offer (email confirmation counts) AND a consent record is created.

2.2 **Payment discipline** (binding on us; we will not deviate in the customer's disfavour):

| Threshold | Terms |
|---|---|
| ≤ R25,000 | Payment before delivery, or agreed short terms |
| > R25,000 | 50% upfront, 50% on defined acceptance |
| ≥ R50,000 | Signed SOW is mandatory (one page minimum: deliverable, date, price, payment terms, acceptance criteria) |
| Subscriptions | Paid in advance |
| Custom enterprise | Priced after discovery |

2.3 **No discounting the same scope.** If price is an obstacle, we reduce scope — we do not discount.

2.4 Invoices are issued same day; payment terms 14 days maximum unless the SOW states otherwise.

## 3. What we provide and prove

3.1 Each Schedule (A–P) defines: scope, deliverables, price, delivery window, acceptance criteria, and the **evidence provided** (receipts, reports, verification records).

3.2 Where a deliverable is a receipt-bearing system, every material system decision or screening outcome is issued a tamper-evident receipt the customer (or a third party they nominate) can independently verify.

3.3 **Honest labelling of capability states**: OBSERVED / VERIFIED / PENDING / UNDEFINED / HYPOTHESIS / VISION. We do not present a hypothesis as a fact.

## 4. Data protection (POPIA)

4.1 We process personal information only to the extent necessary to deliver the engaged service.

4.2 The customer remains the responsible party for their data; we act as an operator/processor where applicable.

4.3 Personal information is not sold, not shared beyond delivery necessity, and retention follows the SOW or the shortest period required by law and the FIC Act where applicable.

4.4 Data subject complaints follow the COMPLAINTS-PROCEDURE.md path.

## 5. Confidentiality & IP

5.1 Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential.

5.2 We retain ownership of our pre-existing platforms (ProofBridge, IVE, STUDI, SEARM); the customer receives the deliverables and licences stated in their Schedule.

5.3 Customer data remains the customer's, exportable on request, deletable on termination subject to legal retention.

## 6. Availability and operations

6.1 Services are monitored on an operating sweep cadence. Incidents affecting the customer are escalated per the escalation ladder in COMPLAINTS-PROCEDURE.md.

6.2 Planned maintenance is notified in advance where the service is subscription-based.

6.3 Receipts and WORM ledger records are retained as the operational evidence base for the engagement.

## 7. Liability

7.1 To the maximum extent permitted by law, our aggregate liability under an engagement is capped at the fees actually paid under that engagement in the 3 (three) months preceding the claim.

7.2 We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, except where the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 or other non-excludable law requires otherwise.

7.3 Nothing in these terms limits liability for fraud or for death/personal injury caused by negligence.

## 8. Term, suspension, termination

8.1 Subscriptions run month-to-month unless the Schedule states otherwise; either party may terminate on written notice per the billing period.

8.2 We may suspend a service for non-payment after written notice and a 7-day cure period.

8.3 On termination: final deliverables are handed over, data is exported on request, and evidence records are retained per section 6.3.

## 9. Complaints and disputes

9.1 Complaints follow COMPLAINTS-PROCEDURE.md (intake, acknowledgment within 1 business day, resolution target 10 business days, escalation ladder).

9.2 Unresolved disputes: good-faith negotiation, then mediation, then SA courts (unless the SOW specifies arbitration). Governing law: the Republic of South Africa.

## 10. Regulatory position

10.1 Where an offering touches regulated activities (FSCA, FIC, SARB, POPIA), the regulatory position is established **from documentary evidence before** the offering is represented as legally operational. Until then, the offering carries PENDING status and is not sold into the regulated use case.

10.2 The current regulatory file is under review (manifest item L7). No regulated-activity claims are made in the interim.

## 11. Amendments

11.1 Material term changes are notified 30 days in advance for subscription services; existing paid terms are honoured to their end.
