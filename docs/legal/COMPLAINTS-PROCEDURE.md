## 1. Purpose

Every complaint is evidence about the system. Handled well, a complaint is free assurance testing. Handled badly, it is reputational and regulatory risk. This procedure exists so that a solo operator in the field never has to improvise a complaint response.

## 2. Intake channels

1. **Primary**: the dedicated complaints email (to be provisioned before first customer handover — currently PENDING).
2. **Secondary**: any channel a customer uses (phone, in person, the workspace) — the receiver logs it into the primary channel the same day.
3. **In-product**: IVE Workspace users can raise a support/complaint event from the workspace; the event is WORM-receipted on creation.

**Rule**: a complaint received anywhere is a complaint recorded — within one business day, always.

## 3. Record (before anything else)

On receipt, record — before investigating:

- date and time received; channel; complainant identity and contact;
- the complaint in the complainant's words (no paraphrasing into softer language);
- the product/service and price-card row involved;
- the consent record id of the engagement, if money has been taken;
- a WORM receipt for the complaint event itself (source: COMPLAINTS).

The record is never edited after the fact; corrections are appended, not overwritten.

## 4. Acknowledgment — 1 business day

The complainant receives: acknowledgment, a reference number, the name of the person handling it (the operator), and this procedure's timelines. No defensiveness, no pre-judgment.

## 5. Classification

| Class | Meaning | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|
| A — Service failure | Deliverable late/missing/below the SOW's acceptance criteria | 10 business days |
| B — Billing dispute | Charge, consent, or price-card interpretation dispute | 5 business days |
| C — Data/privacy | POPIA-related (access, correction, processing) | Per POPIA statutory timelines |
| D — Conduct | Behaviour of the operator/representatives | 10 business days |
| E — Regulatory | FSCA/FIC/ombud-relevant | Immediate escalation to legal review |

## 6. Escalation ladder

1. **Operator** (default handler, days 1–10)
2. **Independent reviewer** — a named neutral third party engaged for the review (to be appointed — currently PENDING)
3. **Legal counsel** (regulatory file review is already pending — manifest L7)
4. **Regulator/ombud** — only after internal paths are exhausted and with legal advice (e.g., the National Financial Ombud Scheme where applicable)

## 7. Resolution

- A written outcome: what was found, what was done, what changes.
- If we were wrong: say so plainly, remedy it, and record the corrective action as a WORM event.
- If we were right: show the evidence — receipts are exactly for this.
- **No settlement that buys silence**: remedies are legitimate (refund, rework, correction); confidentiality only if the complainant requests it.

## 8. Systemic learning

Every class-A complaint feeds the operating sweep's evidence domain. A second complaint of the same class becomes an escalation to the operator by rule — not judgment.

## 9. Metrics (reviewed in the operating loop)

- complaints received / acknowledged within 1 day / resolved within target
- repeat-complaint rate (same class, same root cause = process failure)
- POPIA complaints: statutory compliance, always
